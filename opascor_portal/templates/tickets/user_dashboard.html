How to Submit a Concern
Step-by-step guide on how to report a problem.
  1. On the left sidebar, click Report a Problem.
  2. Select a Category from the dropdown.
  3. Select the appropriate Sub-Category.
  4. Describe your concern clearly (up to 1,000 characters).
  5. Optionally attach a file (screenshot or document).
  6. Click Submit Report.
  7. Your concern appears in the Recent Reports table.

About OPASCOR Portal
Learn more about the OPASCOR Ticketing System.

The OPASCOR Ticketing System is an internal concern management portal for employees of Oriental Port and Allied Services Corporation.

Built to provide a fast, organized, and transparent way to report and resolve workplace concerns.

  • Submit concerns across IT, HR, Maintenance, and Operations.
  • Automatically routes concerns to the right department.
  • Real-time status updates and notifications.
  • Complete concern history for accountability.

Developed and maintained by the MIS Department of OPASCOR.
