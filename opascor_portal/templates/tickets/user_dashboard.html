How to Submit a Concern
Step-by-step guide on how to report a problem.
  1. On the left sidebar, click Report a Problem.
  2. Select a Category from the dropdown that best fits your concern (e.g. IT, HR, Maintenance).
  3. Select the appropriate Sub-Category under your chosen category.
  4. In the description box, clearly describe your concern with as much detail as possible. You may type up to 1,000 characters.
  5. If applicable, attach a file such as a screenshot or document by clicking the attachment area or dragging the file into it.
  6. Once everything is filled out, click the Submit Report button.
  7. Your concern will be submitted and will appear in the Recent Reports table on the right side of the dashboard.
Tracking Your Concern
Learn how to monitor the status of your submitted concerns.

To track your concern, click the Notifications tab on the left sidebar.

Inside Notifications, you will be able to see:

  • The current status of each concern you have submitted.
  • The name of the staff member assigned to handle your concern.
  • Any remarks or updates added by the assigned personnel.
Pending — Concern received, awaiting assignment.
In Progress — A staff member is actively handling your concern.
Resolved — Your concern has been addressed and closed.
About OPASCOR Portal
Learn more about the OPASCOR Ticketing System.

The OPASCOR Ticketing System is an internal concern management portal developed exclusively for the employees of Oriental Port and Allied Services Corporation (OPASCOR).

This system was built to provide a fast, organized, and transparent way for employees to report workplace concerns and for departments to respond to them efficiently.

What this system does:

  • Allows employees to submit concerns across different departments such as IT, HR, Maintenance, and Operations.
  • Automatically routes each concern to the appropriate department for action.
  • Enables department staff to assign, update, and resolve concerns.
  • Keeps employees informed through real-time status updates and notifications.
  • Maintains a complete history of all submitted concerns for reference and accountability.

This portal is developed and maintained by the MIS Department of OPASCOR. For any system-related issues or feedback, please reach out via the Contact IT Support or Email Support options above.
